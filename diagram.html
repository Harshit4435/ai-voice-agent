📞 End-to-End Inbound Call Lifecycle Architecture

The diagram below details the 5 sequential phases triggered upon inbound telephone connection. The AI Receptionist screens the caller, analyzes intent and entities, verifies recipient presence, and automatically branches into either a Live Audio Transfer or a Voicemail Callback Capture.

1
Inbound Greeting
Line connects. AI Receptionist utters initial screening line: "Please state your name and reason for calling."
2
Intent & Entity NLP
Calibrated LinearSVC classifies intent in <2.5ms; extractor extracts Caller Name, Reason, and Urgency.
3
Availability Router
Checks Recipient Status (Alex Mercer): 🟢 AVAILABLE vs 🔴 BUSY / IN MEETING.
4
Callback or Transfer
If Available: Transfers directly. If Busy: Asks for callback phone number & best reach-out time.
5
Alert Dispatch
Dispatches Slack & SMS alert with caller name, reason, phone, and best callback time.
